✳ Ofrenda

A memory altar that never leaves your device.

Record a voice memo from someone you love. Whisper and a small open‑weight model run inside this tab and turn it into a printable remembrance card. No upload, no account, no server that can switch itself off.

Everything below happens on your machine.

04 The card

Download it, print it, or hang it. The link in your address bar carries the whole card — it opens anywhere, no account needed.

Ofrenda runs Whisper and a 0.5B open-weight language model through WebAssembly in your browser. There is no backend. Your recording, your transcript, and the card data stay in this tab (and in the share link only if you choose to copy it).
